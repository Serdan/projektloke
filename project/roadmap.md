# Roadmap

## Current

- Extend the now-public Cass Review / youth-treatment structured pilot with archive/full-text verification: Berlingske, Weekendavisen, Jyllands-Posten and Politiken remain incomplete in open sources.
- Keep corpus roles separate: actor trail, political source material, and structured sample.
- Use primary legal/institutional sources as the baseline when evaluating media framing.
- Continue targeted access-to-information work on youth-treatment capacity, throughput, waiting time, and minor legal-gender outcomes.

## Recently completed / substantially advanced

- Political sequence around B62, B12, B47 and B145, including pre-2023 history and the 2023 EMRK/legal-gender administrative change.
- Dansk Regnbueråd parliamentary uptake and repeated Folkemødet operational/fundraising links.
- Healthcare consultation-response review and youth-treatment evidence map.
- Youth-treatment reorganisation/capacity documentation and access-to-information target list.
- Legal-gender quantitative baseline, including the first public snapshot for minors.
- Prison/changing-room institutional outcomes, education, sport and 2026 party/government updates.
- Facebook hostility baseline from TrygFonden/Analyse & Tal.
- 2026-29 LGBT+ action-plan review, including trans-specific healthcare/research measures and legal-gender limits.
- First structured Cass Review / Danish youth-treatment media pilot, with a fixed 9 April-10 May 2024 window, three verified public sample records and explicit verification limits.
- First-class material-node model for influential reports/guidelines: Cass Review and its separate University of York review package now have distinct nodes, with criticism/rebuttal attached to the document actually evaluated and cross-links from media, rhetoric, timeline and health analysis.

## Next

1. Close the remaining archive gaps in the Cass pilot and full-text-code source plurality, trans/patient voices and headline/body alignment; extend the public sample only as verification improves.
2. Promote other recurring source-only reference points to material nodes when they accumulate meaningful criticism, uptake or cross-domain links; next candidate is Sundhedsstyrelsens 2018 national guidance and its revision trail. Avoid nodes for ordinary one-off sources.
3. Expand the Danish media map beyond the initial Berlingske/Dorte Toft and B47-cited corpus.
4. Research positive/countervailing voices and representation gaps so the project can measure absence/presence rather than only hostile examples.
5. Archive fragile primary sources locally where appropriate and record provenance.
6. Add correction/change history to published records.
7. Add explicit evidence-strength/provenance fields where they improve auditability.
8. Build an outreach package for LGBT+ Danmark and selected editors/reporters once the first structured media sample is stable.

## Later

- Search/filter over the generated public data index.
- Deployment automation.
- Structured correspondence log.
- Broaden beyond transphobia once the trans-focused methodology is mature.
