# Editorial policy

## Purpose

Projekt Loke is an evidence project, not a neutral catalogue of every position. It investigates organized hostility toward minorities and the political, media and activist infrastructure around it.

## Standards

- Quote and characterize people accurately and in context.
- Prefer direct documentation over paraphrase where the exact wording matters.
- Distinguish reporting, commentary, lobbying and formal political action.
- Do not infer private motives from public conduct.
- Do not use false balance to give unsupported claims equal evidentiary status.
- Include meaningful counterevidence and qualifying facts.
- State uncertainty explicitly.

## People and organizations

Profiles should document relevant conduct and relationships rather than become general biographies. Inclusion in the project is not itself a claim that every action or belief of an actor is bigoted.

## Sensitive correspondence

Private correspondence is not publishable by default. Publication requires a deliberate editorial decision taking privacy, consent, public interest and verification into account.
