# Correspondence

Store project outreach and replies here.

Suggested organization:

- one subfolder per organization or recipient when a thread becomes substantial;
- preserve dates and original wording of sent/received messages;
- keep drafts clearly marked as drafts;
- record the source or context that motivated the contact when relevant.

Likely early outreach targets include LGBT+ Danmark and journalists/editors approached about sustained coverage of organized transphobia in Danish politics and media.
